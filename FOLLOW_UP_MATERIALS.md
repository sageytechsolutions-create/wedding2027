## Follow-Up Call (Day 7-8, Thursday)

**Calendar Invite Title**: "Wedding Platform - Your Trial Feedback"

**Duration**: 20 minutes

**Preparation before call:**

1. Review what they did in trial (check activity)
2. Note any issues they ran into (check logs)
3. Prepare feature explanations for what they asked
4. Have pricing/next-steps conversation ready

**Call Structure (20 minutes):**

**Opening (2 min):**
> "Thanks for trying the platform. I'd love to hear what you think—what worked, what didn't, what questions came up?"

**Listen (5 min):**
Let them talk. Take notes. Don't interrupt.

**Clarify (5 min):**
Address confusion:
- "Let me explain how that feature works..."
- "Yeah, that's a bug we're fixing..."
- "That's a great suggestion—definitely adding that..."

**Assess Interest (3 min):**
Ask directly:
> "Based on what you've seen, how interested are you in using this? Are you thinking yes, no, or still deciding?"

**Listen to their answer. Don't oversell.**

**Next Steps (5 min):**

**If HOT (strong interest):**
> "Great. Let's talk about getting you set up on a paid plan. What plan makes sense for your situation?"

**If WARM (interested but needs time):**
> "Totally get it. What would help you feel confident? Another conversation in a week? More feature details?"

**If COLD (not interested):**
> "Fair enough. Can I ask—what would've needed to be different for this to work for you?"
> (This feedback is gold for other prospects)

**Close (take action):**
- Schedule next step (paid plan setup, another call, etc.)
- Send follow-up resources
- Thank them

## Prospect Qualification Matrix

After first demo + trial, score prospects on:

```
| Prospect | Budget | Need | Timeline | Authority | Interest | Action |
|----------|--------|------|----------|-----------|----------|--------|
| John S. | $$$ | High | 30 days | Yes | High | Close this week |
| Sarah C. | $$ | Medium | 90 days | Yes | Medium | Nurture 2 weeks |
| Mike D. | $ | Low | Never | Maybe | Low | Move to follow-up list |
```

**Scoring:**
- **HOT (CLOSE NOW)**: High need, high interest, short timeline, budget
- **WARM (NURTURE)**: Medium interest, longer timeline, open to learning
- **COLD (LIST)**: Low interest or bad fit, keep on list for 6 months
