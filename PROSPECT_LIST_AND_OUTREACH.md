# Prospect List & Outreach Strategy

## Target Profiles (Where to Find Them)

### Profile 1: Active Real Estate Investors
- **Where**: Meetup.com (search "real estate investor")
- **Where**: Local REIA chapters (realtors.org)
- **Where**: LinkedIn (search "real estate investor" + location)
- **Characteristics**: Individual investors with 5-50 properties
- **Pain Point**: Manual tracking, missing deals, ROI calculations
- **Decision Timeline**: Weeks to months

### Profile 2: Portfolio Managers
- **Where**: LinkedIn (search "portfolio manager" + "real estate")
- **Where**: Property management company websites
- **Where**: Commercial real estate networks
- **Characteristics**: Manage 50-500+ properties for clients
- **Pain Point**: Portfolio visibility, performance reporting, scaling
- **Decision Timeline**: Months (enterprise sales)

### Profile 3: Property Managers
- **Where**: Property management associations
- **Where**: LinkedIn search "property manager"
- **Where**: Chamber of commerce directories
- **Characteristics**: Manage investor portfolios, handle day-to-day ops
- **Pain Point**: Portfolio tracking, tenant/property data scattered
- **Decision Timeline**: Weeks to months

### Profile 4: Angel Investors / Venture Capital (Real Estate Focus)
- **Where**: AngelList, Crunchbase
- **Where**: LinkedIn (search "investor" + "real estate")
- **Where**: Industry conferences and events
- **Characteristics**: Actively looking for tech solutions in real estate
- **Pain Point**: Due diligence, market analysis, portfolio performance
- **Decision Timeline**: Weeks (fast movers)

### Profile 5: Real Estate Brokers/Teams
- **Where**: Local brokerage offices
- **Where**: LinkedIn search "real estate broker" or "agent team leader"
- **Where**: MLS association directories
- **Characteristics**: Help clients invest, want added services
- **Pain Point**: Client retention, added value, portfolio tools
- **Decision Timeline**: Months (slow but sticky)

## Your Prospect Research Task

**Find 20-25 specific prospects with:**
- Full name
- Email address
- Company/organization
- Title/role
- How many properties they manage (if known)
- Why they'd benefit

**Where to Start (Pick 3-4 sources):**

1. **LinkedIn**: 
   - Search: "real estate investor" + your city
   - Search: "portfolio manager" + "real estate"
   - Search: "property manager" + industry keywords
   - Save 8-10 profiles, note their connections

2. **Meetup.com**:
   - Search "real estate investor groups" in your area
   - Find upcoming meetings or group members
   - Note organizers and active members
   - Reach out to 3-5 local leaders

3. **REIA Local Chapter**:
   - Visit realtors.org or local REIA website
   - Find member directory
   - Identify 5-10 members (especially officers)
   - Email or find contact info

4. **Google Search**:
   - Search "real estate investment groups near [city]"
   - Search "[city] property management companies"
   - Search "[city] real estate investment firms"
   - Compile 5-10 company websites with contact info

5. **Your Network**:
   - Friends/colleagues in real estate
   - Past contacts who've mentioned investing
   - LinkedIn connections in property/real estate
   - Ask for 3-5 introductions or warm intros

## Email Personalization Strategy

**Always customize with:**
1. **Their name** (Hi [NAME], not "Hi there")
2. **Specific detail** about them (company, properties, role)
3. **Relevant pain point** (what keeps them up at night)
4. **Why now** (why you're reaching out to them specifically)

**Example personalization:**
```
Base: "I noticed you're investing in real estate"

Personalized: "I noticed you're managing the ABC Realty portfolio in Austin, focused on multi-family properties. That scale of portfolio management has unique challenges..."
```

**The goal**: Make them feel you researched them, not that you sent a mass email.

## Pro Tips

1. **Be authentic**: You built this, you're excited about it, and you want feedback.
2. **Low pressure**: "No obligation" removes sales vibes.
3. **Specific value**: Don't say "game-changer"—say what problem it solves.
4. **Your advantage**: You're the founder. Use that credibility.
5. **Follow up**: First email gets 15% response. Follow-up to non-responders gets another 8-10%.
