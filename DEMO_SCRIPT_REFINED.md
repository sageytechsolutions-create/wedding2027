## Pro Tips for Delivery

1. **Let the platform speak** — You don't need to narrate everything. Click through, let them see it work, then explain what matters.

2. **Pause for reactions** — After each major feature, pause. Let them comment. Their reactions tell you what resonates.

3. **Speak to their situation** — Listen for clues about their business (property types, portfolio size, pain points) and emphasize relevant features.

4. **Confidence is key** — You built this. You know it works. Speak with quiet confidence, not apologetic hedging.

5. **Expect technical hiccups** — If something fails to load: "Let me pull this up... [pause]... happens sometimes with loading. Let me show you this screenshot of what that looks like." Then move on.

6. **You're the guide, they're the audience** — You know where this is going. Lead confidently but don't rush.

7. **Eye contact matters** — If in person, look at them when you're not clicking. If virtual, look at camera when explaining, not the screen.

8. **Listen more than you talk** — Your job is 50% demo, 50% understanding their business. Ask questions. Let them talk.

### For a Property Manager:
- **Emphasize**: Transaction tracking, organization, reporting
- **De-emphasize**: Investment analysis
- **Questions**: "What's your current tracking system? What takes most time?"

### For a Casual Investor (2-3 properties):
- **Emphasize**: Simplicity, discovery, AI insights
- **De-emphasize**: Enterprise features and scale
- **Questions**: "What type of properties? How often are you looking to expand?"
