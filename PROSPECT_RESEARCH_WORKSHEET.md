## Research Strategy (Pick 1-2 sources)

### Source 1: LinkedIn (Fastest)
**Why**: Most investors have LinkedIn profiles with email visible or via message

**Search queries**:
```
"real estate investor" + [your city/state]
"portfolio manager" + "real estate" + [location]
"property manager" + [location]

```

### Source 2: Meetup.com (Best for Local)
**Why**: Real estate investor groups list active members

**Steps**:
1. Go to meetup.com
2. Search "real estate investors" + your city
3. Find groups with 100+ members
4. Click on group members
5. Check member profiles for email/LinkedIn

**Time**: 45-60 minutes for 5-8 prospects

### Source 3: Local REIA Chapter (Most Relevant)
**Why**: Most qualified prospects already organized

**Steps**:
1. Google "[your state] REIA chapter"
2. Find local chapter website
3. Look for member directory or officers list
4. Click through profiles to find contact info

**Time**: 30-45 minutes for 3-5 prospects

### Source 4: Google Search (Comprehensive)
**Why**: Finds companies and groups

**Searches**:
```
"real estate investment group" + [city]
"property management company" + [city]
"real estate investment firm" + [city/state]
```

**Time**: 30-45 minutes for 5-8 prospects

## Research Tips

### How to Find Email Addresses

**Direct email on LinkedIn**:
- Click profile → Contact info section → Email listed

**Via LinkedIn message**:
- Send message: "Hi [Name], quick question about your real estate investing. [Your message]"
- Often they'll reply with email

**Company website**:
- Go to company website
- Look for team page or "Contact Us"
- Format is usually firstname@company.com or f.lastname@company.com

**Hunter.io or RocketReach**:
- Free trial can find emails
- Search [Name] + [Company]

**Google search**:
- Search: "[Name] [Company] email"
- Sometimes found in press releases, LinkedIn, or websites

**Ask mutual connection**:
- "Do you know John Smith's email? I want to reach out about something relevant to him"

### How to Verify It's a Real Prospect

**Red flags (skip these)**:
- ❌ Brand new profile (no history)
- ❌ No investment activity visible
- ❌ Only personal use investor (1-2 properties)
- ❌ No response to past outreach

**Green flags (good sign)**:
- ✅ Active in real estate groups
- ✅ Posts about investing
- ✅ Manages multiple properties
- ✅ Listed as officer/manager in organizations
- ✅ Works at property management or investment firm

## Questions to Guide Your Research

As you find each prospect, ask:

1. **Are they a real investor/manager?** (Not just someone who owns their house)
2. **Do they manage multiple properties?** (3+)
3. **Would they benefit from our platform?** (Research/tracking/ROI pain?)
4. **Can I realistically reach them?** (Email findable?)
5. **Are they likely to be interested?** (Their profile suggests they might be)

If YES to all 5 → Add to list

If NO to any → Skip them
